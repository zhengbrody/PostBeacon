# M27.2 — People to contact (beta)

2026-11-28. Adds the "contact people" part of the marketing agent.

## What the founder sees

A **People to contact** card on Diagnose and on Today, and a matching step in the
Marketing agent plan.

1. Add a person: your own label, an optional public link where you will reply, and the
   need they described. Research sources that contain a real quote are offered as
   one-click additions.
2. **Draft messages**: one model request writes a short message for up to five people.
   You can also write each message yourself; that needs no account and no model.
3. Review and edit. A message that reports an unverified result, keeps a placeholder
   such as `[Name]`, or is too long cannot be copied or recorded as sent.
4. Copy it, send it yourself, tick "I sent this message myself", record it.
5. Record what came back, in your own words, or close it as no reply.

## What it does not do

- It does not send messages, post replies or open conversations.
- It does not search for people, profiles, emails or handles.
- "Sent" and "replied" are the founder's report. PostBeacon cannot see the conversation.

Automatic sending was left out on purpose: unsolicited automated messages break the
rules of the communities this product targets and would put the founder's accounts at
risk. Any automatic channel needs its own approval, as Buffer scheduling did.

## Rules enforced by code

- `lib/outreach.ts` owns every transition. Each accepted change bumps the contact's
  revision; a command carries the revision it was issued against, so a double click or a
  late response changes nothing.
- An agent draft never overwrites words the founder wrote.
- "Sent" needs a saved message, the founder's confirmation and a clean truth check
  (`auditDraftSafety`, the same check as public drafts).
- A sent message is kept as sent and cannot be edited.
- The model receives the described need, verified facts, the goal and its signal. It
  never receives the label, the link, the contact id, the goal's target number or its
  time window.
- Stored contacts are normalized on load; an impossible state is downgraded.
- Fictional demo contacts never enter a real project. Re-analysis keeps only people
  already contacted.
- The list is excluded from Copilot requests.

## Persistence

No new table. `workspace.outreach` (at most 20) lives in `projects.meta.workspace`, so
owner-only RLS, account export, account deletion and the 30-day inactivity retention
apply without a new policy. The privacy page lists the new data. Known limit, as for all
workspace state: saves are last-write-wins across devices.

A contact names a third party. The card asks the founder not to record private details,
and the label and link stay out of model requests.

## Model boundary

`POST /api/outreach`: same-origin, signed-in owner required, existing daily metering,
`no-store`, OpenAI only. One request per batch, at most 10 verified facts, 900 output
tokens.
